# Privacy Policy for Tapwheel

**Effective date:** November 27, 2026

MoreFun Labs ("we", "our" or "us") made the mobile game Tapwheel, a free game with ads and optional
in-app purchases. This page explains what data is collected when you play, who collects it, and
what you can do about it.

## 1. What we collect

We do not run our own servers for player data, and we do not sell any data. The game uses
services from Google that collect some data on their own. They are listed below.

### Your game on your phone

Your progress, coins, best scores and settings are saved on your phone. Android may include this
save in your phone's own backup to your Google account.

### Google Play Games (sign-in, leaderboard and cloud save)

The game signs you in with Google Play Games when you have a Play Games profile.

- **Leaderboard.** Your highscore runs are posted to the game's Play Games leaderboard. Other
  players see your score with your Play Games name and picture, if your Play Games profile is
  visible to them. You choose that visibility in your Play Games profile.
- **Cloud save.** A copy of your progress, coins and settings is kept in your Google account
  through Play Games, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or play on another phone.
- Google tells the game your Play Games player ID. We never store it. The game keeps only a
  one-way code made from it, on your phone, to know which scores were posted by whom.

If you do not sign in, nothing goes to Play Games, and your game stays only on your phone.

### Usage statistics and crash reports (only with your permission)

With your permission, Tapwheel sends usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase
(Analytics and Crashlytics). They include your device model, Android version, country from your
IP address, what you do in the game (for example which level you started and how it ended) and
crash details. They do not include your name or your Play Games account.

**They are off until you allow them.** The game asks once, on first launch, and sends nothing
before you answer. You can turn them on or off at any time in Settings > USAGE & CRASH REPORTS.

### Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your
approximate location (country or region from your IP address), and how you interact with ads.
See section 2.

### Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. Google keeps the purchase history. When you open the shop
or buy something, Google Play Billing may also send Google technical details about your phone,
such as its model, Android version and network operator. See section 3.

### The time check

When the game starts, and when you come back to it, it asks our website
(morefunlabs.github.io) for the current time, so daily rewards cannot be changed with the phone's
clock. The request carries no game data, but the site's host, GitHub, sees your IP address, as
with any visit to a website.

## 2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads in the game. In the European Economic Area, the UK and
Switzerland, and in some US states, we ask for your choice through Google's consent message before
any ad is loaded. You can change that choice at any time in Settings > ABOUT & HELP > PRIVACY
SETTINGS (the key shows where the consent message applies).

Buying Remove Ads removes the ads between runs. Ads that you choose to watch for a reward stay
available.

## 3. In-app purchases

All payments are handled by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process or store your card,
bank account or billing details. Google reports purchases to us (for example the item, the price
and the country) for accounting and refunds.

## 5. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

- **On your phone:** uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the save on the phone.
- **Cloud save and leaderboard:** they stay in your Google account until you delete them. You can
  delete the game's Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, in your Play Games
  settings. Google explains how:
  https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646
- **Usage statistics:** Firebase keeps the detailed events for 2 months, and the data linked to
  your installation of the game for up to 14 months after you last play. Crash reports are kept
  for 90 days. Turning them off in Settings stops new data from being sent.
- **Advertising ID:** reset or delete it on your phone in Settings > Google > Ads (the path can
  differ by phone).
- Data held by Google can be viewed and deleted with Google's own tools:
  https://myaccount.google.com

Because we hold no account for you on our own servers, we cannot look up data by your name. If
you have a question about your data, write to us (section 8).

## 6. Children's privacy

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). If you believe a
child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can act on it.
